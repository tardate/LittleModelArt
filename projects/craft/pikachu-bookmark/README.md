# #180 Pikachu Bookmark

Making a Pikachu Bookmark from MDF, cut on an xTool S1 laser cutter.

![Build](./assets/pikachu-bookmark_build.jpg?raw=true)

## Notes

I stumbled upon a [Pikachu Wooden Bookmark](https://www.atomm.com/project/123584-pikachu-wooden-bookmark-pokemon) design
and of course could not resist printing it out.
Just had to adjust the cut settings before printing on the Library's [xTool S1 Laser Cutter](https://leap.tardate.com/equipment/nlb/xtools1/)

![svg](assets/pikachu-bookmark.svg)

The final design file: [pikachu-bookmark.xs](assets/pikachu-bookmark.xs),
